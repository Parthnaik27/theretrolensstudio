Our story

A studio that never quite left the '70s.

The Retro Lens Studio opened its doors as a neighbourhood portrait shop in 1978. It closed for a decade, then reopened in 2019 by a small crew who kept the original enlargers running in the back room.

We still shoot the way the studio always did — real film, hand-processed, printed by people who know your name. The instant booth is new, but everything else is exactly as it was.

Walk-ins welcome Tuesday through Saturday. Sittings and events by appointment.
